Put your media in this folder, then import it at the top of src/config.js and use it in `assets`.
Example:
  import hero from './assets/hero.png';
  import doorVideo from './assets/opening.mp4';
  ...
  assets: { hero, doorVideo, ... }

Sizes:
  poster.jpg     closed doors, portrait 9:16 (first frame of the door video)
  opening.mp4    doors opening, portrait 9:16, 4-6 s, under ~6 MB; last frame = hero image
  hero.png       full invitation artwork, portrait 9:16
  background.jpg       already added (velvet, flowers in two corners) - bride/groom/venue pages
  hero-background.jpg  already added (velvet, flower borders top and bottom) - hero page
  butterfly.png  transparent PNG, ~320 px wide
  song.mp3       background music (loops), starts on "Open Invitation"
  couple.jpg     optional photo on the "Celebrate With Us" page
